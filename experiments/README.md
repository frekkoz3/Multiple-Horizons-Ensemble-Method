# Experiments

This folder contains the empirical evaluation of the proposed method.  
It includes a collection of Jupyter notebooks covering exploratory analysis, methodological insights, and benchmarking experiments.

Although each notebook is self-contained, they are designed to be executed in the following order, as they follow a logical progression of the experimental workflow:

1. **`EDA.ipynb`**  
   Notebook dedicated to the **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** of the five selected benchmark datasets.
2. **`Horizon Aware Loss.ipynb`**  
   Notebook dedicated to the analysis of the impact of the **Horizon-Aware Huber Loss** on the proposed method.
3. **`Ensemble vs Base Models.ipynb`**  
   Notebook dedicated to the visualization and analysis of the ensemble predictions vs the base-models' predictions.
4. **`Evaluation.ipynb`**  
   Notebook dedicated to the quantitative evaluation of the proposed method on the five selected benchmark datasets against the other baseline methods.
5. **`Model Ranking.ipynb`**  
   Notebook dedicated to the ranking of model performances based on the MSE results of the evaluation.
